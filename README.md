# Late Company

A proposed Metro Detroit social coworking lounge and cafe. Concept study dated October 3, 2026.

## Contents
- index.html: public concept site
- plan.html: business model canvas, interactive financial scenarios, location research and plan
- brand.html: visual identity and brand downloads
- late-company-business-plan.pdf: shareable business plan and location research
- late-company-canvas.pdf: one-page business model canvas
- late-company-brand-kit.zip: SVG and PNG logos, color tokens, usage guidance and generated design studies
- economics.json and locations.json: research and model inputs

No venue, opening date or operating approval is represented. Financial figures are labeled planning assumptions unless tied to a cited source. The working brand name is provisional. Generated interior imagery depicts an imagined concept.

The interest form prepares an email draft for the visitor to send. It does not store submissions or send mail automatically.
