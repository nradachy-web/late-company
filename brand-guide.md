# Late Company brand package

Version 0.1. October 3, 2026. Working identity for concept testing.

## The idea
A comfortable social coworking lounge and cafe in Metro Detroit, with 24-hour member access as the ambition. Warmth, easy company, and a place to spend time after other places close.

Main line: **Good company. Even late.**

## Logo system
The companion windows symbolize a room with its lights on and space for more than one person. This is original, editable vector artwork. The mark is geometric path artwork. Wordmark letters use editable Georgia text with Times New Roman and serif fallbacks.

- logo-horizontal-light.svg: golden symbol and oyster wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- logo-horizontal-dark.svg: burgundy symbol and aubergine wordmark for light backgrounds.
- logo-stacked-light.svg and logo-stacked-dark.svg: vertical lockups.
- logo-mono-black.svg and logo-mono-white.svg: single-color versions.
- logo-symbol.svg and logo-symbol-mono.svg: companion-window symbol.
- favicon.svg: simplified site icon in an aubergine square.
- social-card.svg: editable 1200 by 630 concept share card.

Preserve proportions. Leave at least one window's width of clear space on all sides. Use the complete lockup at 160 CSS pixels wide or larger, and the symbol at 24 pixels or larger. Use the symbol rather than a very small complete wordmark.

The wordmark remains live text so it can be edited. Before supplying to a fabricator or printer, use a design application to convert the text to outlines on a copy after checking the installed font. Georgia is a system typeface; no font file is redistributed in this package.

## Color

- Burgundy: #58313D

- Lamplight: #E9B86F
- Oyster: #F4EBDD
- Stone: #C1AFAF
- Moss: #778678

Use oyster for body copy on aubergine and deep burgundy. Use lamplight for a small number of actions or identity moments. Stone is secondary copy. Moss is a supporting visual color, not small body text.

## Type
Georgia is the display voice, with normal weight and close, readable spacing. Use the local system sans serif stack for navigation, forms, and supporting text. This keeps the live site independent of external font requests.

## Image direction
Warm practical lamplight, walnut, burgundy seating, visible coffee, books, and low-pressure social moments. Show quiet work and shared downtime. Avoid nightclub lighting and a sterile office atmosphere.

Generated images are design studies, not pictures of a real venue. Keep the visible AI concept disclosure on the site. The separate logo-exploration.png supplied with the project is an AI-generated mood board; the SVG family is the editable identity used by the website.

## Voice
Conversational, specific, welcoming. Examples:
- Good company. Even late.
- Come with a project. Or absolutely no plans.
- A familiar place. At an unfamiliar hour.

Use simple descriptions for actions. The current form says "Open my interest email" because it prepares an email draft. It does not store a lead or send a message.

## Name screen
Limited web screen on October 3, 2026: searches for the exact phrase with cafe, coworking, lounge, and Detroit did not surface a direct Metro Detroit coworking match. Results did surface an existing Late Company Bar/cafe in Hangzhou, China and the play Late Company. The hospitality use is relevant and needs further investigation.
