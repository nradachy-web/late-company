Late Company is a proposed social coworking lounge and cafe in Metro Detroit. A comfortable place to get out of the house, work on something, find a conversation, or just read around other people.

Bring a laptop. Bring a friend. Come on your own. There would be space for all three.

A few ways to settle in

Find your
kind of corner.

A living room with more people in it.

Soft seats, board games, and a table you can join. Read your book beside someone else's. Catch up with a friend. Let a casual hello turn into an actual conversation.

Planned: lounge seating, shared tables, games, and small gatherings.

Company without the conversation.

Comfortable work tables, convenient outlets, and a quieter part of the room. Bring the side project, the assignment, or the thing you keep meaning to finish.

Planned: a quiet work zone, reliable Wi-Fi, and clear sound boundaries.

Something good in your cup.

Coffee when you need it, tea when you don't, and simple things to eat. The cafe would keep the room feeling welcoming, whether you are settling in or taking a break.

Planned: coffee, tea, nonalcoholic drinks, and a simple food offering. Cafe hours may differ from member access.

These are proposed features. The final space will depend on the location and what the community wants.

A few things
you might be wondering.

Is Late Company open yet?

No. This is a concept in development. There is no announced location, lease, or opening date. Sharing your interest helps us decide what to test next.

Would it really be open all night?

That is the ambition for member access. We need to test demand, staffing, security, building rules, and the economics before committing to it. A smaller evening pilot is a possible first step.

Do I need to be working to be here?

The idea includes time to work and time to simply be around people. The proposed layout separates a quieter work area from the social lounge, so you can choose the evening you want.

What would it cost?

Pricing has not been set. We are exploring a membership alongside options for occasional visits. There is nothing to buy on this site.
